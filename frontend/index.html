Know when to leave for class.

How your departure time is worked out

Transit Buddy picks the class you mean, aims to arrive a few minutes early, then subtracts the estimated trip. Change the numbers to see it move.

  • The trip time is an estimateIt comes from the team's trip planner, which uses Google Maps. Traffic and transit can change it.
  • Home to campus only, for nowPlanning a trip home, or a request with only a leave time, is not supported yet. The bot tells you so.
  • Your building, not a guessIt knows 56 ASU Tempe buildings. If yours isn't one of them, it uses the Memorial Union and says so.
  • Your mode of travelIt uses your preferred way of getting to campus when you've saved one: driving, bus or train, walking or biking. Otherwise it assumes a car.

How it works

Three stops

Add the bot

Invite Transit Buddy to your Discord server. Save your home address in your private commute profile so it can estimate trips.

Upload your schedule

/schedule upload reads your classes from a screenshot. You check them and press Confirm. Nothing is saved before that.

Ask when to leave

/commute plan turns a plain request into a departure time. /ask answers other questions about your schedule.

In development

Next stop: classmates

Matching and shared departure times are built but not switched on yet, so no command offers them today.

  • Not live yetMeet on your wayOnly students who opt in are compared. Matches need the same area, day, direction and campus, and departure estimates within about 15 minutes. The plan is to keep names hidden until both sides accept.
  • Not live yetTemporary and consent-basedMatch data is kept in memory for two hours and cleared on restart. Schedules alone never create a match.

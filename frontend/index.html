Same class. Same bus. Same ride.

Transit Buddy reads your class schedule from a screenshot, then helps you and your classmates agree on when to leave for campus and when to head home. It is a bot in your own Discord server.

Privacy

Your address stays yours

Matching is opt-in. Flip the switch to see exactly what a classmate gets when they run /commute match.

  • Private setup form/commute setup opens a form only you can see. Replies to your commands are private too.
  • Area, not addressClassmates see a general area and an approximate time window. Your home address is only used for your own trip times.
  • Nothing saved without a yesA schedule screenshot is shown to you first. It is saved only when you press Confirm.
  • Delete any time/commute forget-address removes your commute profile, including the address.
How it works

Three stops

Add the bot

Invite Transit Buddy to your server. Run /commute setup once to save your area, usual departure time and whether you want matches.

Upload a screenshot

/schedule upload reads your classes from an image. You check them and press Confirm. Nothing is saved before that.

Agree on a time

/commute availability, /commute match and /commute plan turn everyone's classes into one departure time to propose.

Next stop: your server

Free to add. You can remove the bot from your server whenever you like.
